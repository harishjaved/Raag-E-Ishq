Our Story & Vision

When One Girl Learns, an Entire Community Rises.

Founded in Kerala, Joygivers is a volunteer-driven social initiative bridging financial and digital barriers for girls from underserved households. Too many capable young students leave classrooms early due to textbook costs, commute hurdles, or lack of mentorship.

By uniting compassionate citizens, educators, and cultural patrons through soulful community events like Raag-E-Ishq, we turn every seat and contribution into sustained academic support from primary school through college.

"Education is not charity — it is a girl's rightful foundation for dignity, confidence, and independence."
Joygivers Grassroots Trust Kochi, Kerala
